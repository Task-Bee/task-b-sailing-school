Memory bridge

Wind feels forward on your cheek. The boat climbs upwind, but not straight into it.

Sail trim

Sails in tight. The boom stays close to the centreline and the genoa is pulled in.

How to use this model

The wind is fixed from the top. Drag the boat left or right, or choose a point of sail above the model. The compass and sails update to show how the boat sits relative to the wind.

No-go zone

The red zone is the area too close to the wind. In this zone the sails lose drive and the boat may slow, stop, or drift backwards.
